# Hero Dirt privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Hero Dirt doesn't collect, sell, or share your personal data. There are no accounts, ads, or third-party analytics.

**Location.** If you allow it, your location is used on your device to center the map. It is not stored or sent to the developer.

**Saved places.** The places you save are stored on your device and in your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage) so they sync across your devices. The developer can't access your iCloud data.

**Weather and soil lookups.** To show conditions for a place, the app sends that place's coordinates to these services, which return weather or soil data:
- [Apple WeatherKit](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) (rain forecast)
- [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) (recent rain, evaporation, rain map)
- [ISRIC SoilGrids](https://www.isric.org/about/privacy-policy) (soil texture)

No other information about you is included in these requests.

**Diagnostics.** The app keeps simple usage counts and Apple's crash/performance reports (MetricKit) on your device only. Nothing is uploaded.

**Contact.** Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/irodrigo17/hero-dirt-ios/issues.
